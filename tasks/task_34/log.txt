MESSAGE:
Phase 2 was a naming-and-coupling cleanup, and the brief had two places where
the stated fix contradicted the stated goal. I checked both against the code
before touching anything rather than implementing them as written.

The first: 2.4 says to replace the hard-coded location/world colours with
"--role-supporting and --role-rival", then in the same breath says to confirm
the chips match the entity cards. They cannot both hold. The entity views do not
use those tokens — views/locations.js and views/worlds.js hard-code the same two
hex values for their pips, and components.css's .tag-location uses the same teal.
So --role-supporting is the token for the supporting *role*, and roleColor
('supporting') returns exactly that teal: a location chip would become
indistinguishable from a supporting character, and REL_TYPE_COLORS.ally is the
same colour again. Three kinds would have collapsed into one colour, which is the
defect this phase exists to remove. I put one table in colors.js
(ENTITY_KIND_COLORS, beside ROLE_COLORS and REL_TYPE_COLORS, which JS_ORDER loads
first) and pointed entityRef and both views' pips at it. Four copies became one.

The second: 2.1 says to add the assertion to "the node/JS check", but no such
check is in the repo. Phase 1's 44 checks are a scratch file, and Phase 4.2 is
what gives them a home. I added it there and made it stronger than asked — it now
asserts every path's deepestRung is null or a number in 0..3, not just paths[0],
and it fails on the word form. The harness also had its own stale assertion on
the old field name, updated with it.

2.2 offered a choice and I took the rule. It is not decoration: core/constants.py
already states "the same character on both sides means inner conflict" as a
checkable fact, and the class was load-bearing — it is what made the arrowhead
land at the turn rather than on the answering bubble. While verifying that I
found the head was positioned at xb unconditionally, which for a self-directed
arrow is a coordinate the path never reaches; it now sits at the turn.

Verification was DOM assertions, not screenshots — the vision tool was down, and
the assertions are stronger evidence anyway. The rendered SVG shows the inner-
conflict link as M 50 0 C 94 1 94 21 50 22 with its head at 94, against the two
exchange arrows at M 25 ... 75 22 and back. 41 node checks, 25 headless-Chrome
checks, full suite green.
