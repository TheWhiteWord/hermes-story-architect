# Arc beats

**Covers.** Writing the chain. A beat is anchored to a character, pinned to a scene, and ordered — that is what makes an arc a chain rather than a list. Action → gap → choice → shift; crisis and climax flags.

**Not in it.** The thirteen field names. What a beat is in general (`theory/`).
