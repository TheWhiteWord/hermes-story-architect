### Storytelling and Didactism

```yaml

    definition: creative demonstration of truth

    failure: creative defeat
    ideal: show, don't tell
```

### Plot Definition

```yaml
  definition: writer's chosen sequence of events and their design in time
```

### Story Triangle Designs

```yaml
plot:
  designs:
    archplot:
      causality: events linked by cause and effect
      protagonist: active, goal-oriented
      reality: consistent fictional world rules
      protagonist_count: single
      conflict: external
      time: linear, chronological
      ending: closed (all questions answered, emotions satisfied)
    miniplot:
      ending: open (questions unanswered, emotions lingering)
      conflict: internal
      protagonist_count: multiple
      protagonist: passive, reactive
      derivation: reduction of archplot elements
    antiplot:
      causality: coincidence, random events, no causal links
      time: nonlinear, disrupted or fragmented
      reality: inconsistent, shifting or illogical rules
      derivation: reversal of archplot elements
```

### Triangle Contrasts

```yaml
plot:
  contrasts:
    - axis: ending
      archplot: closed (resolution, all questions answered, emotional satisfaction)
      miniplot: open (ambiguity, unanswered questions, lingering emotions)
    - axis: conflict
      archplot: external, outward struggles
      miniplot: internal, inner battles, external conflicts still present
    - axis: protagonist
      archplot: active, drives plot forward
      miniplot: passive, reactive; inner journey or external events compensate
    - axis: time
      archplot: linear, chronological, flashbacks integrated clearly
      miniplot: linear, chronological
      antiplot: nonlinear, fragmented, scrambled (beginning/middle/end not necessarily in order)
    - axis: causality
      archplot: cause-and-effect, interconnected events
      miniplot: cause-and-effect, interconnected events
      antiplot: random events, disconnected episodes, open ending
    - axis: reality
      archplot: stable world rules, consistent interaction modes
      miniplot: stable world rules, consistent interaction modes
      antiplot: shifting realities, illogical jumps, absurdity
```

### Inciting Incident

```yaml
plot:
  inciting_incident:
    definition: radically upsets the balance of forces in the protagonist's life
    reaction_required: true
    functions:
      - disrupts balance
      - arouses desire for balance
      - provides object of desire (physical, situational, attitudinal)
      - triggers conscious and unconscious desire
  timing: early, when the moment is ripe
  quality: germane to world, characters, genre
  minimum_scale: may be subtle
  checklist:
    - radically upsets balance
    - arouses desire to restore balance

    - raises major dramatic question
```

### The Gap

```yaml
plot:
  gap:
    origin: born where subjective and objective realms touch
    trigger: protagonist pursues object of desire, acts on expected positive reaction
    reaction: unexpected, more powerful or different than anticipated
    source_levels: [inner life, relationships, extra-personal world]
    composition: objective necessity vs subjective probability
```

### Gap Progression

```yaml
plot:
  progression:
    - step: action_1
      event: protagonist acts, expects result
    - step: gap_1

    - step: adjustment
      event: protagonist revises reality perception
    - step: action_2
      event: riskier action based on revised reality, expecting new result
    - step: gap_2
```

## scene

### Exposition Rules

```yaml
scene:
  exposition:
    principle: show, don't tell
    dramatized: present necessary facts indirectly through natural scenes, dialogue, behavior
  backstory:
    power: previous significant events revealed at critical moments create turning points
  flashback:
    rules:
      - dramatize flashbacks as active scenes
      - introduce only when the audience has need and desire to know
  audience_knowledge:
    mystery: audience knows less than characters
    suspense: audience and characters know the same information
    dramatic_irony: audience knows more than characters
  coincidence:
    rules:
      - bring in early to allow meaning to develop
      - never use to resolve the ending (deus ex machina)
  pov:
    law: more time with a character = more witnessed choices = greater empathy
```

```yaml
scene:
```

### Scene Analysis Technique

```yaml
scene:
  analysis_steps:
    - step: 1
      name: define_conflict
      detail: identify who drives the scene and motivates actions
      unit: beat (action/reaction)
    - step: 2
      name: note_opening_value
      detail: value at stake and its positive/negative charge at scene start
    - step: 3
      name: break_into_beats
      detail:
        - analyze first action on text and subtext levels
        - name subtextual action as active gerund phrase (emotive, not literal)
        - describe reaction as active gerund phrase
      rule: beat continues until behavior changes
    - step: 4
      name: note_closing_value_and_compare
      outcome:
        no_change: nonevent, flat scene (exposition only)
        change: scene turn
    - step: 5
      name: survey_beats_locate_turning_point
      detail: review gerund phrases for arc; locate moment of major gap opening
      turning_point: moment expectation and result diverge
  defect_modes: [no conflict, repetition, misplaced turning point, lack of credibility]
```

## sequence

### Progression Types

```yaml
sequence:
  progression_types:
    - id: social
      direction: widen impact of actions into society
    - id: personal
      direction: deepen actions into intimate relationships and inner lives
    - id: symbolic_ascension
```

### Crisis

```yaml

      - maximum pressure on protagonist
```
