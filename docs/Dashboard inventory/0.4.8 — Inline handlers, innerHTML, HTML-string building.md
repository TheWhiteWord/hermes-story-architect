**Two patterns dominate.** (1) The eight `show*Panel` functions in
`entity-panels.js` each assign `panel-body` or `panel-footer`

act seg and chart stack from `innerHTML` assignments (`:502`, `:521`,
`:571`), plus the crumb strip (`:409-413`) and the act button strip
(`:537`). Together these two files are 26 of the 61 sites.
