### Answers to questions

**Unit 3 (`script-view.js`).** No dependency on `DASH.openStatsPanel`, `track-data.js`, or the
tab strip. `DASH.boot()` at `:106` is app-shell work (Phase 2), not part of unit 3 —
`initStory` builds all nine views at once (`core.js:100-108`); unit 3 is the screenplay
view only. Unit 3 does call `DASH.showScenePanel` (unit 8) at line 91 to open the scene
panel when a heading is clicked.

**Unit 5 (`views/story.js`).** Calls `DASH.showScenePanel` (unit 8) at lines 54, 57 and
`DASH.showPlotPanel` (unit 8) at line 81 via inline `onclick` handlers. The scene cards
also emit `data-ent` attributes (`scene:${id}`) which is the data protocol, not a call.
The plot cards carry an inline `onclick` to `DASH.showPlotPanel`.

**`renderSectionsHtml` callers.** 8 call sites: 7 in `entity-panels.js` (:66, :141,
:223, :306, :514, :556, :626) and 1 in `scene-beats-tab.js` (:161). Matches the plan's
assumption exactly. Note: `panel-tabs.js` (161 lines) has NO `renderSectionsHtml`
callers — line 161 in the earlier draft cited the wrong file.
