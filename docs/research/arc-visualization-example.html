What the Graph Reveals

Slope (Δy/Δx): Mara's arc has a steep drop between scenes 45→82 (Betrayal → Crisis), showing rapid deterioration. Oak's arc declines more gradually — his corruption is revealed slowly, then collapses at the end.

Inflection point: Mara's curve bottoms at the crisis (scene 82), then rises slightly to the ironic climax. This is the hallmark of an arc that doesn't end at the absolute lowest point — she regains something (justice) but loses everything else.

Divergence: At scene 4, both characters occupy similar value space (trust). By scene 94, they're maximally apart — their arcs are mirror images, showing the relationship's destruction.

Over-drama check: Mara's |Δy| per beat: 0.5, 0.7, 0.5, 0.3. No single beat exceeds 0.8. This is a measured decline, not hysterical. Oak's are even smoother: 0.2, 0.8, 0.5, 0.2.

Beat Data (stored alongside the graph)

Each beat stores the linguistic turn + derived Y value:

- Action: Mara asks Oak about the discrepancy. Gap: He lies smoothly — she realizes months of lies. Choice: She nods, files the report herself. Shift: Trust → Doubt (positive → contrary). Y: +0.3

The Y value is derived by the LLM at authoring time. The dashboard plots Y directly. No separate derivation pipeline.

Dashboard Sanity Checks (applied at render time)

If all |Δy| < 0.2: Arc is flat — nothing happens. Flag to user.

If any |Δy| > 0.8: Single beat is melodramatic. Suggest splitting into two beats.

If two arcs never diverge: Characters may be redundant — suggest differentiation.

If irony flag set but Y < 0: Ironic positive requires Y near +1 with irony=true. Mismatch = authoring error.
